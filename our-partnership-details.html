Strategic Higher Education Consulting That Moves Institutions Forward

  • FA Solutions partners with Mac Insight Group to support institutions navigating enrollment, revenue, financial aid, leadership, and operational challenges.
  • Mac Insight Group provides strategic consulting across enrollment and student success, financial aid and revenue strategy, institutional leadership, systems, data, and operations. Their approach is grounded in partnership, practical execution, and measurable outcomes.
  • Through this partnership, FA Solutions helps connect institutions with experienced higher education consultants who can bring clarity to complex challenges and help turn strategy into sustainable results.
  • Mac Insight Group is a higher education consulting firm specializing in enrollment strategy, financial aid optimization, student success, and institutional transformation. Through our partnership, institutions gain access to a powerful combination of operational excellence and strategic insight. Together, we help colleges and universities move beyond transactional financial aid processes to create data-informed strategies that strengthen enrollment, improve student outcomes, and drive long-term financial sustainability.
